The hub is the whole-film timeline (scenes sized by runtime, shots as ticks) over a scene slip and reel list. Scope is a brushed range. A scene opens as its timeline, where split, merge and reorder are done on the cells.

The hub is the Master Narrative itself, scene by scene, with a margin slip per scene. A scene opens as text blocks with shots bracketed against the passages they cover; gaps and overlaps are visible, shots expand in place.
