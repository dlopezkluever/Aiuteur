## Product model and philosophy

- Target user and automation: the PRD names marketing teams as a target user and frames the core value as "automating" narrative-to-film ‹src: project-overview› VS the mission rejects marketing/UGC ad factories and any automation that takes over authorship; agents only speed up low-care tasks ‹src: post-Aiuteur Poduct Mission & Philosophy› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-001, WD-IDEA-PHIL-018, WD-IDEA-PHIL-023, WD-IDEA-PHIL-024, WD-IDEA-PHIL-035)
- Pro users and precision: "true animators/filmmakers" with their own storyboards who want precise control are named as targets, and the README promises filmmakers who "demand creative control" ‹src: Tickets, master-mvp-tasklist, README› VS the mission deliberately gives up highly technical users, and features only the top ~10% want are "designed incorrectly" ‹src: post-Aiuteur Poduct Mission & Philosophy› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-018, WD-IDEA-PHIL-019, WD-IDEA-PHIL-021)
- Editing-suite feel: the UI should feel like professional editing software (Resolve, Premiere) ‹src: ui-and-theme-rules› VS the product is explicitly not an Adobe replacement ‹src: post-Aiuteur Poduct Mission & Philosophy› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-029, WD-IDEA-PHIL-021)
- Long-term ambition: a "world-class" platform with professional-grade features comparable to industry tools ‹src: Post-MVP-tasklist› VS a happy middle that never competes with Adobe-style professional suites ‹src: voice-message-5-2, post-Aiuteur Poduct Mission & Philosophy› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-031, WD-IDEA-PHIL-020, WD-IDEA-PHIL-021)
- Continuity as a promise: narrative continuity is one of two core promises, The Inversion "guarantees deterministic consistency", and all continuity issues must be resolved at checkout ‹src: master-mvp-tasklist, project-overview, golden-test-evaluation-framework› VS perfect continuity and accuracy are explicitly not the goal; quick, funny, and true to intent is ‹src: voice-message-5-2› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-001, WD-IDEA-PHIL-006, WD-IDEA-FLOW-027, WD-IDEA-PHIL-028)
- Deterministic rules vs reasoning: determinism over magic, with explicit inspectable rules ‹src: inheritance_contract› VS deterministic rules cannot reliably turn unstructured user prompts into structured output, so a reasoning step must interpret intent ‹src: voice-message-5-2› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-043, WD-IDEA-PHIL-044)
- Streamline audience: expert users need a structured input to "fly through", and Streamline serves power users with existing scripts ‹src: Totally-New-Features, Tickets› VS Streamline's users are laymen, not power users, needing progressive disclosure and plain language ‹src: streamlined-input-analysis-5-10› (related: WD-IDEA-INPUT-038, WD-IDEA-PHIL-065, WD-IDEA-PHIL-019)
- Device focus: desktop-first, mobile low priority ‹src: missing_todos-not-sure› VS the roadmap plans full mobile, tablet, and native apps ‹src: Post-MVP-tasklist› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-030; see WD-IDEA-ALT-004)

- Build order: feature-completion first, then quality ‹src: MVP-tasklist-v1.1› VS each stage done "as well as possible", favoring correctness over speed ‹src: MVP-tasklist-v1.1› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-089, WD-IDEA-PHIL-090)
- Fully autonomous mode: "let AI cook" is questioned as no different from a general agent and against the anti-takeover mission ‹src: Totally-New-Features, post-Aiuteur Poduct Mission & Philosophy› VS the same note marks it "NOT OUT [OF] SCOPE", and YOLO-style intake lets AI take broad creative liberties when input is thin ‹src: Totally-New-Features, Yolo-mode› (related: WD-IDEA-PHIL-026, WD-IDEA-PHIL-024, WD-IDEA-INPUT-041)
