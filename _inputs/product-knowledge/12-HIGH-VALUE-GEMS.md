# 12 High-Value Gems

> Scope: a deliberately selective set of unusually inventive, nuanced, or easy-to-lose ideas from the legacy corpus. Not a v2 recommendation, not a priority list, not an MVP cut. Each entry names its WD-IDEA id (with the KB document that holds the full statement) and why it is substantive. Legacy product thinking, not a description of current behavior. Legend: see 00-README.

## Product stance and philosophy

- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-006** (see 01) The Inversion (plan, anchor with cheap frames, generate video last) is the core economic and continuity insight the whole pipeline is built on.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-013** (see 01) The "middle 80%" defines the user as much by who is excluded (one-click users and Adobe pros) as by who is included.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-025** (see 01) A contrarian thesis that artists using video models do not want agents to take over sets the product apart from the dominant trend.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-024** (see 01) A hard scope rule: any feature that makes unsanctioned creative decisions is out, however popular.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-083** (see 01) The four-question North Star test turns the mission into a usable filter for every feature.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-084** (see 01) Says outright that too many well-working features still hurt the product by obscuring what it is.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-034** (see 01) Flexible pacing (speed through what you don't care about, slow down where you do) explains the recurring default/advanced mode pattern.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-009** (see 01) The "creative compiler" metaphor explains global-to-local narrowing and why small upstream edits must have deterministic downstream effects.
- **WD-IDEA-PHIL-072** (see 01) A cross-project creative fingerprint / second brain is the boldest long-term moat idea in the material.

## Workflow spine, gating, shortcuts

- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-021** (see 02) Making script approval the deterministic, AI-free Phase A to B handoff with stable scene identities is what lets later edits repair scenes rather than rebuild them.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-042** (see 02) A live "ballooning" counter against target runtime turns an AI tendency to over-generate into a visible, user-controlled tradeoff.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-059** (see 02) The three-way Stage 12 resolution (complete, minor visual redo, major narrative return) encodes cost-proportional repair routing.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-065** (see 02) Recasting Stage 10 from a generation endpoint into the main continuity and curation workspace is a bold reframe of where creative control lives.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-067** (see 02) Script revision reconciliation (diff, keep untouched scenes, re-enter only changed ones) solves the real-world second-draft problem without forking.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-074** (see 02) "Land users as far as their material earns, with upstream clearly marked" is a crisp, reusable principle for every shortcut path.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-078** (see 02) Treating "skipped" as a real terminal state that counts as passed lets shortcuts coexist with strict gating.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-091** (see 02) Matching storyboard panel labels (1A, 1B) to script labels is a simple, inventive bridge from rough sketches to structured shots.
- **WD-IDEA-FLOW-132** (see 02) "Fix the chain before amplifying it" (references must reach the generator before prompts get better) is a high-leverage sequencing insight.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-041** (see 02B) Scaling creative liberty to how much material was supplied is an elegant single principle for when to follow inputs and when to invent.

## Intake and source material

- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-008** (see 02B) The "Video Content for Audio" project type flips the pipeline: an existing timestamped audio track drives a dialogue-free visual story.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-039** (see 02B) Building Streamline as a general intake pipeline rather than a script shortcut keeps every later input type an extension, not a bolt-on.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-057** (see 02B) The spatial intake canvas ("organizing a desk, not filling out a form") avoids stacking a second wizard on a 12-stage pipeline.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-081** (see 02B) Always-visible classification confidence turns silent misclassification, the most noticeable failure, into a collaborative question.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-088** (see 02B) Separating "no reformatting pass" from "touched by clarification edits" is a subtle trust-preserving distinction most designs would blur.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-109** (see 02B) Storyboard-label matching (1A, 1B, 2A) could skip Stages 2–4 and turn shot listing into distillation, a whole alternate pipeline entry.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-111** (see 02B) Ask Auteur reframes intake from a file parser into an assistant that asks targeted questions instead of guessing.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-123** (see 02B) The re-ask rule handles contradictions arriving weeks later with append-only history instead of silent overwrite.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-124** (see 02B) An explicit source-of-truth ranking (answer > primary > context > inference) gives a deterministic way to settle conflicts.
- **WD-IDEA-INPUT-126** (see 02B) Review-before-commit as a v1 hard requirement roots the whole feature in trust: nothing a user supplied is acted on invisibly.

## Writing and story

- **WD-IDEA-STORY-034** (see 05) Recasting the screenplay as a "visually verbose blueprint" makes the script the image-generation spec, not a theatrical document.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-069** (see 05) Highlight-to-rewrite with a custom instruction gives surgical, context-aware control over every text artifact.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-071** (see 05) The rule that surrounding text is read-only context is the core guarantee that user-approved prose survives AI edits.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-026** (see 05) Tracing each beat back to its source prose keeps structural edits grounded in the narrative.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-101** (see 05) Bidirectional, non-cascading staleness flags make drift visible without ever rewriting the user's work automatically.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-104** (see 05) Reverse sync (beats re-extracted from the script) lets the downstream document become the source of truth when the user prefers.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-082** (see 05) Variable-scope reconciliation (word patch, paragraph rewrite, structural cascade) scales the AI's edit to the size of the answer.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-114** (see 05) Freezing kept scenes during a partial story pivot protects finished production work while the rest of the story is rewritten.
- **WD-IDEA-STORY-126** (see 05) The Lore Library imagines the story world as a persistent, growing asset across a series.

- **WD-IDEA-ASSET-030** (see 06) The influence hierarchy (reference image beats style capsule) names the core tension of visual consistency and how to resolve it.
- **WD-IDEA-ASSET-032** (see 06) A user-facing capsule influence slider turns the style-vs-identity tradeoff into a direct creative control.
- **WD-IDEA-ASSET-060** (see 06) Surfacing script contradictions in asset descriptions instead of silently resolving them respects authorial intent.

- **WD-IDEA-ASSET-118** (see 06) Edit Image sends only the requested change plus the current image, avoiding description overload, a sharp insight about how image models behave.
- **WD-IDEA-ASSET-119** (see 06) Apply Visual Style turns a real photo of yourself into the project's aesthetic, making personal casting trivial.

## Continuity and creative state

- **WD-IDEA-CONT-156** (see 04) The continuity "trifecta" (reference image, frame analysis, canonical asset text) names which failure each source prevents, a clean design principle for any reference-driven generation.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-028** (see 06B) 
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-027** (see 04) Media stages have no creative authority, so defects get fixed upstream, never patched at render time; this keeps the pipeline deterministic.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-073** (see 04) The scene end state has three named consumers (shot list, asset relevance, frame prompting), which makes the scene-to-scene handoff a concrete contract.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-124** (see 04) Splitting work between transformations (which appearance applies) and chaining (action, position, state) stops two systems from fighting over the same truth.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-038** (see 04) Re-injecting established facts as "do not contradict" on every call protects earlier user decisions from drift in regenerations months later.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-180** (see 04) Shot-reverse-shot shows that continuity is a graph, not a sequence, which drives the whole non-adjacent link design.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-147** (see 04) The honest fidelity ladder (small reframes OK, medium hit-or-miss, POV and reverse unreliable) sets realistic expectations for camera-change features.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-047** (see 04) Condition tags with carry-forward (WD-IDEA-CONT-065) model temporary physical state apart from identity and wardrobe, so muddy stays muddy until fixed.
- **WD-IDEA-CONT-239** (see 04) The creator's 9-scene pivot scenario is a sharp acceptance test: revise the middle or end without losing anything already produced.

- **WD-IDEA-LOC-017** (see 06B) The resolution of many iterations: one structured system, two depths of engagement, plus optional blocking.

- **WD-IDEA-LOC-075** (see 06B) Lets the story itself tell the user which spatial references are missing, with no manual tagging.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-156** (see 06B) Removes the setup dead end: the shot list invents the needed directions so users can fill images later.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-186** (see 06B) An honesty rule: degrade visibly to text rather than borrow a wrong place.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-192** (see 06B) Turns a mismatched reference into a usable one by telling the model exactly how to adapt.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-230** (see 06B) The self-reinforcing loop where production output becomes the reference, turning early hallucinations into canon.

## Shots, cinematography, previs

- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-112** (see 07) The CineBlock core bet (an ~8-shot scene blocked in under a minute, steering generation) is the single hypothesis on which the creator says the whole pipeline's viability rests.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-102** (see 07) A clean division of authority between blocking still (framing, angle, crop), location reference (environment identity) and asset references (appearance) addresses the observed failure where generic wide references drag tight shots back to wide framings.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-055** (see 07) "Stretch" split semantics turn the generator's fixed clip limit into a creative lever: splitting adds screen time instead of halving it.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-091** (see 07) Asset presence types (throughout / enters / exits / passes through) map in-shot staging directly onto which references feed the start frame, end frame or video only.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-035** (see 07) The static-versus-moving camera split (frame prompt gets where the camera is; video prompt gets how it moves; rack focus is temporal) is a precise, reusable rule for two-stage image-to-video generation.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-044** (see 07) Defining action as what a viewer sees (blocking, visible body language, spatial relations) and banning "they talk" makes the shot list a usable visual blueprint rather than a story summary.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-069** (see 07) An "Auteur" shot-style capsule, distinct from the visual style capsule, treats directing grammar (shot count, types, sequencing) as a selectable style.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-073** (see 07) Film sampling borrows another film's editing, blocking and pacing and re-expresses them in the user's own story and look, a bold creative-input idea.
- **WD-IDEA-SHOT-071** (see 07) Suggesting experimental techniques from a compiled memory of legendary shots pushes the AI beyond default CU/MS coverage at a user-controlled frequency.

## Generation and prompting

- **WD-IDEA-GEN-004** (see 08) The "looks vs happens" test is a simple rule for splitting every shot into an image prompt and a video prompt, and most of the prompt architecture follows from it.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-011** (see 08) Because first-frame video models cannot take references, all environment fidelity has to be won at the frame stage. This explains why frame quality decides video quality.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-063** (see 08) The disambiguation test ("would removing this detail make the action ambiguous?") is a crisp, teachable exception to the lean video prompt rule.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-026** (see 08) Showing every assumption the AI makes, instead of inserting it silently, turns prompt automation into a trust feature.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-025** (see 08) Turning abstract emotion into concrete physical cues is the core skill that makes generated performance readable.
- **WD-IDEA-LOC-200** (see 06B) 
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-174** (see 08) The composition-authority rule splits the job between a blocking still (framing) and a location image (look), which resolves a common conflict between reference images.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-074** (see 08) Automatically adding "no subtitles" to dialogue shots is a small, high-leverage fix for a known model hallucination.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-272** (see 08) No paid render starts without a credit reservation, a budget and an execution ceiling. This defense in depth makes agentic generation financially safe.
- **WD-IDEA-GEN-277** (see 08) Capping retries per shot with an "accepted best effort" state stops runaway agent spend while still delivering output.

## Performance, audio, editing

- **WD-IDEA-AV-010** (see 09) Letting a recorded ad-lib override the script reverses the usual flow from text to performance, so a human performance becomes the source of truth.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-030** (see 09) The "Voice-Actinator" is a compact standalone product: one frame, one action, and a voice performance produce a matched animated clip.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-028** (see 09) Keeping the emotional inflection while swapping the voice print splits performance from timbre, so anyone can voice any character.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-034** (see 09) Audio-first projects invert the whole pipeline, the clearest statement that this would be a separate product mode.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-048** (see 09) Sorting review issues by type, with an agent that maps each one to the upstream stage that can fix it, turns watching a cut into targeted repair.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-066** (see 09) "Unzip and start cutting within minutes" is a concrete, testable success bar for the whole export.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-067** (see 09) A deterministic export makes the delivery package reproducible and diffable, which professional handoff depends on.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-070** (see 09) All-or-nothing validation means no editor ever receives a broken timeline.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-019** (see 09) Keep, refine, or replace native audio is a practical policy for treating generated sound as a draft, not a final.
- **WD-IDEA-AV-022** (see 09) A voice seed in every video prompt treats voice as a persistent character asset, just like appearance.
