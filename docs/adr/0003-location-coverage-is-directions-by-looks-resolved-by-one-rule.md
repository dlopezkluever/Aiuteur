# Location coverage is Directions by Looks, and a shot's views are resolved by one rule

Status: accepted (Wayfinder #53); amended by Creator Ratification Record 2 D59 (Wayfinder #59) and by Creator Ratification Record 6 §3 (L6.1–L6.6), which control. The text below states the current decision; the amendment notes at the end record what changed.

A Location must be seen from many angles. A Location starts with one canonical reference image, and coverage follows the shots: the views the planned shots need become its coverage, generated from the canonical reference and cheap to accept in bulk. The creator may add more views at any time. A single image is a per-location choice, and shots must know their location and the direction or directions needed to cover the space they reveal, without manual management (Constitution §6.4 as amended by Record 2 §8 and Record 6 L6.2). V1 modelled views as rows with their own camera fields, resolved a shot's location through a fuzzy resolver with stored confidences, and had two classifiers that disagreed on the threshold (legacy 07 §§2.7, 10.2, 10.3).

V2 makes **Coverage** part of the Location: a set of **Directions** (named places to look from, with an optional opposite), each with a **View** Slot per Look (or the base), filled by Attempts (generated, uploaded, or promoted from an approved Frame) with one active. Single-image mode is coverage with one Direction. Coverage is a sparse matrix: Views for a Look are made only when a shot needs that Direction in that Look. A Direction keeps its label, description and optional opposite, and is not assumed to be an unrelated image: nothing prevents relating Directions to a later Location spatial model (LATER, Constitution §15.3; L6.6).

A Location starts with one approved **canonical reference image** (its identity, which is its base Look) and **no Directions are proposed up front**. **Shot planning resolves each shot to one or more ordered Directions**, picking existing named ones or proposing new named ones, as many as are needed to cover the space the shot reveals. The criterion is the revealed space, never a camera-movement label: a static wide shot may need several, a small pan may need one (L6.3). A new Direction's empty View becomes a **coverage need**. Views are generated from the canonical reference only when the creator asks (no automatic media spend, Record 2 X8). They arrive as draft Attempts and are accepted in bulk, reversibly. The creator may add Directions or generate more Views at any time, including before any shots exist. Two readiness levels are derived and never gate (X9): **Identity readiness** and **Production coverage readiness** (every resolved Direction of each shot, by Look, whose Slot has no View yet). A location variant may be an alternate Look or a separate Location entity. The creator chooses, and neither is privileged (D59.6).

A shot refers to its **Direction Slots**, never to View Attempts, so selecting a different active View never breaks it. A shot's Directions are shot-owned: changing, adding, removing or reordering them never raises write-back (Record 2 P2). Resolution is one rule: the location is the setting Mention at the shot's anchor (explicit beats derived; states are explicit, derived or unresolved, with no stored confidence). The ordered Directions are the creator's explicit ones, else the AI's proposal from the shot's cinematography persisted as an editable AI-made choice (the default, with no advanced setting), else the primary one if coverage has one (single-image mode's one Direction). With no Direction, the ladder starts at the identity image. Each Direction's View follows a fixed ladder down to the identity image and then text only. Falling down the ladder is reported quietly and never blocks; so is a single-image Location whose one image covers less than the shot reveals (a quiet reference-quality note, L6.5). Nothing generates until the creator starts the priced coverage action. When an Offering accepts fewer location references than the shot resolved, Fit chooses the subset that best covers the revealed camera path, never an arbitrary one, and reports a reference-quality warning when the path cannot be fully covered (L6.4; method spec-level).

## Considered options

- **Views as per-shot images.** Rejected: contradicts "shots know their direction without manual management" and loses frame reuse as a normal path.
- **Resolver confidences and thresholds stored on shots** (V1). Rejected: two consumers applied different thresholds; the only things worth storing are the creator's explicit choices.
- **Exactly one Direction per shot** (this ADR before Record 6). Rejected: a wide or moving shot can reveal more than one View covers, and the generator then has no reference for part of the place.
- **Choosing the number of Directions from a camera-movement label** (pan, dolly, static). Rejected: a static wide shot may reveal several Directions and a small pan only one; the revealed space is the criterion.
- **Re-deriving the AI's Direction choice on every read.** Rejected: it would silently change when unrelated inputs change; persisting it as an AI-made choice makes it visible and overridable.
- **Sub-locations borrowing the parent's Views.** Rejected: a kitchen is not the house's exterior. The parent's identity image is only a "same place" reference for generating the sub-location's own coverage.

## Consequences

- Image models do not document camera-angle re-rendering from a reference (research #6, 2026-10-18), so every generated View is an Attempt the creator reviews, regeneration of one Direction is cheap, and Frames can be promoted into coverage. The model depends on no specific provider.
- Coverage need is known only once shots exist, so it is derived from the shot plan, not predicted. Rejected: inferring needed Directions from Mentions before shots exist (the creator chose identity first, coverage from the actual shots later).
- Coverage quality varies without breaking shot resolution, because the ladder always yields something.
- The Location's coverage lives inside the Location, so copy-on-use takes it along.

## Amendment (Record 2 D59, 2026-10-22)

As first accepted under #53 (detailed in `ENTITIES-AND-LOOKS.md` §5.1 at that time), the AI proposed each Location's Direction set up front from its description and the Master Narrative (for a room, typically its walls plus an establishing view). All Directions were then generated in one batch during World Definition. The creator replaced this with identity first and shot-driven coverage (D59.1, D59.5, D59.11), and Constitution §6.4 and §15.1 were amended to match. The structure, the one-rule resolution, the fallback ladder and R5 are unchanged; only when Directions come into being changed.

## Amendment (Record 6, 2026-10-25)

Until Record 6, a shot resolved exactly one Direction. Creator Ratification Record 6 §3 (L6.1–L6.6) and the 2026-10-25 Constitution amendment (§6.4, §15.3, Appendix B) changed this: a shot resolves **one or more ordered Directions**, as many as the space it reveals needs (never a camera-movement label). The AI proposes them at shot planning as an editable AI-made choice, by default and with no advanced setting; nothing generates until the creator starts the priced coverage action. Fit chooses the reference subset that best covers the camera path when an Offering accepts fewer, and warns when the path cannot be fully covered. Single-image mode stays, with a quiet note when the shot reveals more than the image covers. Location spatial layout is LATER, and Directions must not be modelled as unrelated images. The old up-front "multi-direction by default" is not restored. The structure, the sparse Direction × Look matrix, the Direction-Slot reference, shot ownership, the fallback ladder (now applied per Direction) and R5 are unchanged.
